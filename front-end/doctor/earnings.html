Earnings & listing plan

What you earned through NexCare, what the platform charged, and whether a different tier would cost you less.

How NexCare charges you. Two parts: a monthly listing fee, and a commission taken from each consultation you actually complete. The cheaper the listing fee, the higher the commission — so a busy practice pays less by moving up a tier, and a quiet one pays less by staying on the free tier. Nothing is charged on an appointment that was cancelled.
