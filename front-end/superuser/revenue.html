NexCare makes money in eight ways, from three different payers. Hospitals buy the platform, doctors buy visibility, patients buy convenience. Everything below is derived at read time from the underlying bills, appointments and dispatches — nothing is stored pre-aggregated, so a total can never drift away from what actually happened. What a hospital collects from its own patients is that hospital's revenue and is shown to its manager, not counted here.
